## Selection patterns

- Wallet or payment object: `wallet-growth`, `calendar`, `chain-link`.
- Soft savings or container object: `money-bag`, `wallet-growth`, `storefront`.
- Screen, chart, or analytics object: `market-chart`, `calendar`, `external-link`.
- Market-action object: `candlesticks`, `market-chart`, `wallet-growth`.
- Metallic or commodity object: `bullion`, `chain-link`, `calendar`.
- Minimal abstract symbol: `external-link`, `chain-link`, `calendar`.
- Empty state: prefer geometry and material cues from `external-link`, `calendar`, `chain-link`, or `storefront`, but suppress all blue and accent colours.

The user’s image controls **what** is depicted. Approved references control **how** it is depicted. Empty-state colour rules override colours visible in every reference.
